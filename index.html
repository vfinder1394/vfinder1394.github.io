Lokale KI Upload

A private, single-user tool of the YouTube channel “Lokale KI” (German explainer Shorts about running AI locally). It uploads the channel owner's self-produced videos to that one channel and schedules their publication. It has no other users.

▶ This tool uses YouTube API Services

It requests only the youtube.upload permission. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
